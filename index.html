ABOUT US

Trivedi Associates is a construction and project management consultancy firm established in 2016, founded on the principles of professional integrity, technical excellence and a commitment to delivering value-driven solutions.

Founded by Mr. Aakash Trivedi, Founder & Chairman, a Designer, Civil Engineer with an MBA in Construction Project Management, the firm brings together technical knowledge, strategic planning and a structured approach to construction and project execution.

At Trivedi Associates, we believe that successful projects are built on more than just drawings, materials and structures. They require thoughtful planning, transparent decision-making, efficient resource management and seamless coordination among all stakeholders.

Our approach focuses on aligning design intent, quality, cost and timelines to achieve project objectives while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and accountability. We strive to create lasting value for our clients by combining engineering fundamentals with modern project management practices.

As we continue to grow, Trivedi Associates remains committed to building enduring relationships, embracing innovation and contributing to the creation of efficient, sustainable and well-executed built environments.
